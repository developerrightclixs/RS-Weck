Professional Experience

Commercial real estate valuation often requires far more than identifying comparable sales and applying standard valuation techniques. It requires the ability to recognize, analyze, and interpret the many factors that influence value, including market conditions, income and expenses, capitalization rates, highest and best use, financing considerations, and the economic characteristics of the property. By combining decades of hands-on experience with proven quantitative and qualitative analysis, I am able to deliver insightful and superior results to my clients.

Local Experience

Nothing can replace local knowledge of the marketplace. I have been appraising commercial properties in Northern California since 1986. While 80% of this work has been conducted in the Four-County Sacramento area, the remainder of my assignments are divided between the Central Valley, the San Francisco Bay Area and even Los Angeles on occasion.

My extensive experience allows me to provide valuable context that goes beyond the numbers and to articulate my analysis in both written form and verbally when necessary. I can bring attention to specific market conditions, trends, and other matters of importance that may influence the evaluation and ultimate conclusion of value for a particular asset. My goal is to provide practical, market-driven insight that helps others better understand not only what a property is worth, but why.

Complex assignments require a depth of understanding that often times involve knowledge and insight that is only acquired over years of professional practice. The projects below represent a few such assignments completed over my career.

Senior Living

Portfolio of Luxury Assisted Living Facilities

CalSTRS sought an As-Is appraisal on a 13-facility joint venture with one of California's largest operators. Property locations included Laguna Hills, Mission Viejo, Beverly Hills, San Ramon and Walnut Creek. Analysis included a Discounted Cash Flow Analysis with the value exceeding $600 million.

Mixed-Use

700 Broadway, Santa Monica

In 2022, this site was improved with an older 35,000 Von’s Market situated on an acre of land seven blocks from the Pacific Ocean. Reliant Companies, a major national development firm obtained entitlements to construct an 8-story tax-credit apartment project, a new 60,000 square foot Von’s Market with two-levels of underground parking, a bowling alley and a premier Equinox Health Club.

Master-Planned Community

Nine Mile Ranch, Tehama County

Planned as Del Webb’s 3rd retirement community in Northern California this property became a casualty of the 2008 recession. Pulte walked away from a $20 million investment after securing entitlements for 3,700 residential units, an 18-hole championship golf course, and 230,000 square feet of commercial development. With a 20-year development agreement in place, Robin Weck appraised this property based on the existing entitlements.

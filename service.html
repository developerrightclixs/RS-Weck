A Restricted Appraisal Report is a type of real estate appraisal report prepared in accordance with the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP) that provides a limited presentation of the appraiser's opinions, analyses, and conclusions.

A Restricted Appraisal Report provides a more abbreviated presentation and relies more heavily on the reader's familiarity with the property, assignment, and appraisal process.

A Full Narrative Appraisal Report is a comprehensive written presentation of a real estate appraiser's research, analysis, reasoning, opinions, and conclusions, prepared in accordance with the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP). A full narrative report provides a much more detailed explanation of the appraiser's methodology, supporting data, market analysis, and reasoning behind the value conclusion.

The Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP) establishes the nationally recognized standards governing appraisal development and reporting and serves as the benchmark throughout all 50 states for evaluating an appraisal report's credibility, reliability, and compliance with accepted professional standards.

An Appraisal Review is an independent, objective examination of another appraiser's work, including the appraisal report, underlying data, analyses, methodologies, assumptions, and conclusions, to determine whether the work is credible, adequately supported, and compliant with applicable professional standards.

Under the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP), an appraisal review is an examination of an appraisal or appraisal service performed by another appraiser, with the purpose of developing an opinion about the quality of that work.

The reviewer's responsibility is not simply to determine whether they agree with the original appraiser's value conclusion, but to evaluate whether the appraiser's research, reasoning, methodology, and conclusions are reasonable and adequately supported.

Partial Interest Valuation

In real estate appraisal, determining the value of a partial interest requires more than simply applying the ownership percentage to the value of the whole property. The key issue is identifying what rights are actually owned and how those rights affect marketability and control. Identifying the key components provides the foundation of partial interest valuation problems. For a typical undivided fractional interest the analysis can be summarized as follows:

  • Pro Rata Ownership Share
  • Adjust for rights and restrictions
  • Consider control characteristics
  • Consider marketability
  • Arrive at value of Partial Interest
